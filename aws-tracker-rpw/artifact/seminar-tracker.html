How the filters are decided

Tampa area is an 813, 727, 863, 941 or 352 phone number. Rest of Florida is any other Florida area code. Out of area is a phone number outside Florida — they get the emails, but there is no point calling them about a Saturday morning in Tampa.

No phone is email-only, so they can never be called at all. Held out is deceased or do-not-contact: they are excluded from every count and every export on this page.

The list is sorted with the newest clients first, by the date their file was opened.

This page is the live record. Every status, call tick, note and send total you type saves to a shared database within a second, and everyone else looking at the page sees it without reloading.

The client list is not part of this page. The page itself holds no names, numbers or addresses — it is an empty board until someone loads the CSV, and the list then lives in this tracker's own database, readable only by people you have given the link to and who are signed in. Street addresses, dates of birth and case notes are never read from the file at all.

Phone numbers and emails are shown as plain text with a copy button rather than as links, because dialling and mail links do not work reliably inside a hosted page.
